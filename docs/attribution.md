# Attribution

Event material must distinguish project-authored explanations from third-party facts and assets.

## Required for factual content

- Official or identified source URL
- Accessed or event year
- Any material qualification that narrows the source claim

## Required for third-party assets

- Asset owner or creator
- Original source URL
- Relevant license or reuse basis when known
- Any required attribution wording

Record repository-wide exceptions in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). Put event-specific details beside the source data or in that event's README.

Do not infer that a brand, logo, screenshot, video, or event asset is reusable merely because it is publicly visible.
