# Code of conduct

Event Atlas welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience. Be respectful, assume good intent, give specific technical feedback, and keep discussion focused on the work.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sustained disruption, unwanted contact, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, decline contributions, limit participation, or block accounts when necessary to protect the project and its community. Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's repository contact mechanisms.

Contributors are expected to comply with GitHub's [Acceptable Use Policies](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies) and the applicable event community standards.
